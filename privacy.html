Privacy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Your files never leave your device

Every Q-FT tool (photo enhancement, PDF compression, image conversion, audio and video trimming) runs inside your web browser. Your files are not uploaded to any server, and we never see them.

What we store

Your browser keeps a small counter of how many AI enhancements you have left (in local storage on your device). There are no accounts, no tracking cookies and no ads.

Payments

Enhancement packs are paid through Stripe. Stripe handles your card details under its own privacy policy; Q-FT never sees or stores your card number.

Third-party services

To load the tools quickly, the site downloads code from public content networks (jsDelivr) and fonts from Google Fonts. Like any website, these services can see your IP address when your browser fetches those files.
