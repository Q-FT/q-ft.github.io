Questions, answered

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Every tool runs inside your browser on your own device. Your photos, PDFs and videos are never sent to a server, so they stay private.

Why is AI enhancement limited when the other tools are free?

You get 10 free enhancements. After that, packs are $5 for 20 photos. It's a one-time payment that keeps Q-FT running, not a subscription.

What does "Enhance" do?

It uses Real-ESRGAN, an AI model trained to restore detail: it upscales your photo 2× or 4× while removing blur, noise and compression artifacts. Great for old, small or blurry photos.

Will compressing a PDF keep the text sharp?

Yes. Q-FT only recompresses the pictures inside the PDF and tidies up its structure. Text and drawings stay crisp and selectable.

Is trimming video lossless?

In "Fast" mode, yes: the clip is cut without re-encoding, so there's zero quality loss (the cut lands on the nearest keyframe). "Precise" mode cuts on the exact frame and re-encodes at high quality.

It's slow on my computer. Why?

All the work happens on your device. AI enhancement uses your graphics card when the browser supports it (Chrome, Edge, recent Safari) and is much slower without it. Big videos also take longer.
